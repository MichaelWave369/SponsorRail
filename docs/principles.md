# SponsorRail Principles

## FUNDING != AUTHORITY

Money may grant compute. Money may not grant control.

SponsorRail separates the funding plane from the execution plane so that a sponsor can pay for useful computation without gaining access to private task data or influence over agent cognition.

## Non-negotiable invariants

1. A blind sponsor does not receive the user prompt.
2. A blind sponsor does not receive repository or source context.
3. Sponsor identity does not enter model context.
4. Sponsor instructions do not enter model context.
5. Sponsorship is optional for the user.
6. Funding and compute usage are auditable.
7. Receipts must not contain private prompt, source, or output data.
8. Sponsorship must add capability, not manufacture inconvenience.
9. Funding must never silently alter recommendation ranking.
10. Disclosures must be plain and recognizable as sponsorship.

## Product rule

> Someone can pay for your AI to work. Nobody buys the right to tell it what to think.
